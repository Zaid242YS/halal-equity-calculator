Coach's rationale: why equity is measured this way
  • Equity = cash you put at risk. Total funding (capex + working capital) minus debt. Lenders are paid first, so owners absorb every shock.
  • NPV of equity cash flows at your cost of equity shows whether the farm beats your next-best investment.
  • IRR and equity multiple show speed and size of return on invested cash.
  • Exit equity value = final-year EBITDA × multiple − remaining debt + working capital recovered.
  • DSCR (cash earnings ÷ debt service) tests whether the farm survives a bad year. Below 1.2x is a warning.
  • Monte Carlo replaces a handful of hand-picked cases with thousands of random price, yield, feed and capex draws. It turns "what if" into "how likely".
